Privacy Policy

CivLive · effective 21 October 2026

CivLive is a live-streamed simulation run by its channel owner. This policy explains what information the stream software handles and why.

What we access

Using the channel owner's Google authorisation, the software reads the live chat of the channel's own broadcasts through the YouTube Data API, creates, starts, retitles and ends the channel's own broadcasts, and answers viewers' information commands (such as !me, !top and !help) with short replies in that broadcast's chat, posted from the channel's own account. It does not upload videos, or access any viewer's Google account or any other channel's data.

What we use and keep

  • Chat commands (such as !vote and !name): the display name, channel ID and command are used in memory to count votes, collect name suggestions, enforce cooldowns and address replies (a reply mentions the viewer's display name in the broadcast's chat). A suggested name that viewers pick may be given to a place or people in the simulation and shown on stream. Commands are not stored after the stream.
  • Predictions: viewers who join a prediction are recorded under a one-way hashed ID (not the channel ID itself) with their moderated display name and points. Points reset each calendar month; the previous month's top predictors are kept and may be shown on stream.
  • Super Chats and Super Stickers: the event ID, display name, amount and currency as reported by YouTube, and any message, are stored so each one is thanked exactly once. The message is deleted as soon as the thank-you is given, and the remaining record is deleted after 30 days.
  • Adopted villagers: a Super Chat can adopt a villager or bring a small blessing to the simulation. The supporter's moderated display name and a one-way hashed ID are saved with the simulation for as long as it runs, so their villager (and that villager's descendants) can be credited on stream. A villager with a remarkable life may be remembered in the stream's Hall of Legends.
  • Operational logs record technical events (such as connection errors). They do not record chat messages.

Service providers

  • OpenAI: game state, and viewers' moderated display names, name suggestions and Super Chat messages, may be sent to OpenAI to generate narration and to check messages for harmful content.
  • YouTube / Google: the stream and chat are hosted by YouTube under YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

The narrator's voice is generated on the channel owner's own computer; narration is not sent to a voice service. We do not sell or share information for advertising, and we do not use it for any purpose other than running the stream.

Google API Services

CivLive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your choices

You can watch and take part without sending commands or Super Chats. To ask about, or request deletion of, a stored Super Chat record, an adopted villager's credit or your prediction points, contact us with your display name and the approximate time.
