Privacy Policy

AI Civilization Live · effective 19 October 2026

AI Civilization Live is a live-streamed simulation run by its channel owner. This policy explains what information the stream software handles and why.

What we access

Using the channel owner's Google authorisation, the software reads the live chat of the channel's own broadcasts through the YouTube Data API, and (if enabled by the owner) creates and ends the channel's own broadcasts. It does not access any viewer's Google account.

What we use and keep

  • Chat commands (such as !vote): the display name, channel ID and command are used in memory to count votes and enforce cooldowns. They are not stored after the stream.
  • Super Chats and Super Stickers: the event ID, display name, amount and currency as reported by YouTube, and any message, are stored so each one is thanked exactly once. The message is deleted as soon as the thank-you is given, and the remaining record is deleted after 30 days.
  • Operational logs record technical events (such as connection errors). They do not record chat messages.

Service providers

  • OpenAI: game state, and Super Chat display names and moderated messages, may be sent to OpenAI to generate narration and to check messages for harmful content.
  • ElevenLabs: narration text, which may include a Super Chat sender's display name and amount, is sent to ElevenLabs to produce spoken audio.
  • YouTube / Google: the stream and chat are hosted by YouTube under YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or share information for advertising, and we do not use it for any purpose other than running the stream.

Google API Services

AI Civilization Live's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your choices

You can take part without sending commands or Super Chats. To ask about or request deletion of a stored Super Chat record, contact us with the approximate time it was sent.
