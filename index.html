AI Civilization Live

A persistent pixel-art civilization simulation, streamed live on YouTube.

Three or more factions share a procedurally generated valley of mountains, rivers and forests. They gather, build towns, trade, research technology, suffer plagues and natural disasters, and go to war. Each faction is led by an AI ruler with its own personality who chooses a strategy every few in-game days and explains the decision on stream. Narration is also AI-generated.

Taking part

  • !vote <faction> gives a faction a small, temporary boost (one vote per minute).
  • !stats <faction> shows that faction's statistics; !leaderboard, !status and !help also work.
  • Super Chats and Super Stickers are thanked on stream. They never buy in-game outcomes.

How it works

The stream is produced automatically by a program that reads public live chat through the YouTube Data API using the channel owner's authorisation. It does not post to chat or act on viewers' accounts.
