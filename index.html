The ladder

From one wanderer to empires

Every people climbs by growing, discovering and settling, and falls back when it loses towns or people. No one is handed a rung.

  1. WanderersAlone, foraging and looking for company
  2. BandThree who bond share a camp and a leader
  3. TribeTwelve or more, with Fire and Language
  4. VillageFarming lets a tribe settle its first town
  5. ChiefdomTwo towns, armies and treaties
  6. KingdomWriting, three towns and a crown
  7. EmpireSix towns and the top of the ladder

Taking part

Chat can sway the valley

Type commands in the live chat. They're free.

  • !1 !2 !3Vote in the open round: an omen, a name, or a ruler's decision
  • !name <idea>Name a new band or town
  • !predict <choice>Call what happens next and climb the monthly leaderboard
  • !root <name>Back a hero or a villain
  • !storyThe storylines unfolding now
  • !helpEverything else

The people decide

When a ruler faces a bold choice, such as war or peace, or crushing a revolt or giving in, chat may get a minute to choose for them. If chat votes, its choice stands.

Omens

Every so often chat picks an omen for the valley: gentle rain, a mild winter, plentiful herds. Busy chat wakes the spirits sooner.

Super Chats

Super Chats and Super Stickers are thanked on stream. One can adopt a villager to follow through their life and family line (!adopt), or send a small blessing. These only tilt the odds a little; they never decide wars or wipe out a people.

Honest by design

Real stories, told as they happen

Nothing is scripted

Storylines open, turn and close only on things that really happen in the simulation. Even quiet stretches end by the world's own rules.

AI, and it says so

The rulers' choices and the narration are AI-generated, and the stream says so on screen. When the AI is unavailable, a rule-based council decides.

How the show runs

The stream is produced automatically by a program running on the channel owner's computer. It manages the channel's own broadcasts and reads public live chat through the YouTube Data API using the channel owner's authorisation, and answers information commands like !me with short replies in chat. It never acts on viewers' accounts.
