CivLive

An AI civilization that evolves on its own, streamed live on YouTube.

▶ Watch live on YouTube   Read the rules of the valley →

A procedurally generated valley of mountains, rivers and forests starts with a few scattered people. Over days of streaming they pair up, raise families and form bands, which grow into tribes, villages, chiefdoms, kingdoms and empires. They discover fire and farming, mine and work metal, invent new tools and weapons, develop cultures and faiths, trade, and go to war. Each people is led by an AI ruler who chooses a strategy and explains it on stream, and an AI narrator tells the story as it happens. Remarkable lives are remembered in the Hall of Legends.

Taking part

  • Polls and omens: vote in chat polls (!1 !2 !3) to send a small omen, such as rain or a mild winter.
  • Naming: when a new band forms or a town is founded, suggest a name with !name <idea>, then vote for a favourite.
  • Predictions: call what happens next with !predict and earn points for the monthly leaderboard (!top).
  • Super Chats and Super Stickers are thanked on stream. A Super Chat can adopt a villager to follow through their life and family line (!adopt), or send a small blessing. These only tilt the odds a little; they never decide wars or wipe out a people.
  • !help lists everything else, and the full guide explains every command.

How it works

The stream is produced automatically by a program running on the channel owner's computer. It manages the channel's own broadcasts and reads public live chat through the YouTube Data API using the channel owner's authorisation, and answers information commands like !me with short replies in chat. It never acts on viewers' accounts.
